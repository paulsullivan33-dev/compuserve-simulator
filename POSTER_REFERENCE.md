# Photographed CompuServe menus

The default TOP menu now follows all twelve choices in IMG_3286.jpeg, including
the prompt `Enter choice number !`. `GO POSTER` also browses these categories.
The HELP menu and FIND A TOPIC menu follow IMG_3289 and IMG_3290. Direct commands are
`GO COMMUNICATE`, `GO NEWS`, `GO SHOPPING`, `GO MONEY`, `GO GAMES`, `GO HOME`,
`GO COMPUTERS`, `GO BUSINESS`, `GO HELP`, and `GO INDEX`.
`M` returns to the previous menu; `GO TOP` returns to the top.

`GO QUICK` opens a searchable, paginated directory. `S words` searches, `F`/`B`
page through results, `ALL` clears a search, and a displayed number opens a topic.
The same directory backs the INDEX screen's search and list options. This is a
reconstructed index of this build's destinations, not a transcription of the
poster’s complete alphabetical index.

The computer menu's first four choices now open photo-transcribed menus:
Software Forums (PCS-10, PCS-4), Hardware Forums (PCS-20, PCS-6, PCS-8),
Magazines/Electronic Newsstand (MAGAZINES), and Science/Technology (PCS-40,
PCS-41). Sources are IMG_3305 through IMG_3308. Enter or F advances a page;
B goes back a page; M returns to the parent menu. Page changes replace the
current list page rather than accumulating submenu levels. Direct GO page
addresses and relative PCS addresses also work.

The exact printed numbering is preserved, including software choices 11-16,
hardware choices 9-20, and the unexplained science gap between 9 and 11.
World of Lotus (W) is preserved without guessing what W means. Every forum and
publication destination opens a related reconstructed community, identified
as a reconstruction before opening. Online Today opens Online Weekly as its
related periodical reconstruction; the latter remains available directly
through GO MAGAZINE.

The 31 readable quick words from IMG_3287 and IMG_3288 remain searchable through
GO QUICK and stored with their source filenames in poster_words.json. No new
historical quick words are inferred from menu titles.

HELP connects the tour, command summary, What's New, access-number directory,
profile settings, feedback, billing, and the reconstructed subscriber directory. Online Today and Rules of
Operation still need implementation and reference
material. Online Today has not been silently replaced with Online Weekly.

The original simulation menus remain available through `GO NEWSSIM`,
`GO GAMESSIM`, `GO TRAVELSIM`, `GO REFERENCESIM`, `GO FINANCE`, and `GO SHOP`, including features added to the
simulation that do not appear on the poster.

Sources: user-supplied IMG_3285 through IMG_3292, and IMG_3294 through IMG_3299.jpeg.
Each transcribed screen records its source filename in screens.json. Labels,
numbering, fee markers, page keywords, and visible continuation lines follow
the photographs. The terminal retains its configured colors: poster ink and
paper colors are not evidence of the historical terminal palette.

Existing simulated services are reused where applicable; their internal screens
are not claimed to be transcriptions. Services not recreated display an explicit
notice. Fee markers are historical text, not newly implemented charges.
